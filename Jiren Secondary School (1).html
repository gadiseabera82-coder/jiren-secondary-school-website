A school that knows every student

Jiren Secondary School opened in 1980 and has served the people of Jimma for many years. It is a community of teachers, students and families working together. We teach the national curriculum with care, including Natural Science and Social Science, and we make room for sport, clubs and service so students learn far beyond the textbook.

Our teachers stay close to each learner, notice who needs help, and celebrate every step forward.
